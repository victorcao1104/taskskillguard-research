# Phase 12: Skill-security integration of Phase 5 and Phase 6

This is an additive return to the original Skill-security research question.
The Phase 5 final framework is the foundation; Phase 6 supplies executable
truth checks and equal-capability recovery controls. Previous results and
papers remain unchanged.

Start with [the study README](studies/skill-security-v12/README.md),
[the Chinese analysis](studies/skill-security-v12/REPORT_ZH.md), and
[the rewritten IEEE report](studies/skill-security-v12/output/paper.pdf).
The new experiment uses synthetic Skill packages only. It includes source-grounded
effects, scope and flow enforcement, legal recovery, model/graph ablations,
retained first failures, and zero-API reproduction.
