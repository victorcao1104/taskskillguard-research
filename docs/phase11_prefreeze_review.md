## Findings requiring resolution before the stated freeze

1. **[P1] Freeze can silently omit the promised experimental analysis.** `scripts/phase11_common.py:23` includes `scripts/experiment_phase11.py` in glob patterns, but that file did not exist at review time; nonexistent patterns are silently omitted. The design names matched-prefix replay, family aggregation, an oracle lower bound, and primary contrasts without their executable implementation. It also leaves “strongest predeclared heuristic” and “correct ... or explicitly retain descriptive intervals” unresolved. Before claiming a prospectively frozen analysis, create and include the analysis implementation or an exact analysis specification fixing the primary comparator, estimands, failure/missingness handling, and inference choice. Require named mandatory freeze inputs to exist. If analysis is intentionally developed after observing results, label it exploratory instead of retrospectively calling it predeclared.

2. **[P2] Decision attempts are reported as requests even when no provider request occurs.** `scripts/run_phase11.py:107` counts every decision record, and `finalize()` reports `requests=len(decisions)`. A synthetic invocation raising `BUDGET_LIMIT` before provider I/O produced `decision_count=1`, `stop_reason=budget_exhausted`, and zero cost with zero provider calls. Thus the promised actual-call metric is wrong on a supported stop path. Distinguish decision attempts, confirmed provider sends, pre-dispatch budget rejection, and ambiguous interrupted intent. An interrupted dispatch marker cannot by itself prove a provider send. Preserve the conservative unknown-usage cost, but do not present it as observed billing or an observed call count.

3. **[P2] The acquisition grid does not enforce its statistical unit.** `scripts/run_phase11.py:131` checks 192 streams and 24 case IDs, not 24 distinct families with one selected variant per family. Constructing 24 distinct `*-0` case IDs all belonging to one family yields 192 streams and 24 case IDs, passing that predicate with **one family**. Enforce the family count, one selected case per family, unique stream IDs, and the complete per-family model/view/stop grid before acquisition. This is a validation gap; it does not assert that the withheld author actually supplied malformed data.

4. **[P2] Budget-truncated acquisition is not scheduled deterministically as documented.** `scripts/run_phase11.py:166` assigns entire adaptive streams to four workers. Submission order is deterministic, but later requests and budget reservations depend on provider latency and when workers finish earlier streams. Under a binding shared cap this changes which families/cells are observed; a lock prevents overspending but does not remove this censoring. Resolve before acquisition by proving a conservative bound sufficient for the complete declared grid, using a deterministic budget allocation/dispatch rule, or explicitly declaring the latency-dependent incomplete-grid design and its missingness analysis. Do not claim deterministic request scheduling from the current implementation. Predeclare how comparisons handle streams stopped by the cap.

## Mechanisms assessed as coherent within their stated assumptions

- Positive certificates require the same route to have paid successful guarded-run evidence in every compatible world. Initial observations narrow the class; run queries do not reveal the actual world. The trusted-store verifier recomputes binding and witness coverage without interpreting routes. The revised unavailable-observation handling does not manufacture intrinsic infeasibility when an unanswered observation could still split the class.
- Selectors see public structure and acquired records; the heuristic does not invoke a free semantic interpreter. Public partition scoring is legitimate information, but its CPU work and structural-cost traversal are excluded from current interpreter/check counters. Explicitly report that exclusion or measure it before calling those counters total computation.
- The paid audit and fault-free recovery executor share the bounded walker, guarded dispatch, exact goals, and receipt reuse. Existing tests include stateful reads, explicit receipt-key conflicts, denied calls, and shared quotas. The independent withheld semantic comparison remains necessary; this review cannot substitute for it.
- Recovery preserves read receipts and retries the same chosen route. Exact authenticated committed status imports a receipt; fenced terminal noncommit licenses a fresh generation. Absence/pending/unavailable/mismatched evidence does not license safe-policy continuation. The delayed-original unsafe control illustrates why authenticated absence alone is insufficient. Status queries consume both shared calls and separate status budget, and retries are bounded.
- The commit entry point deliberately does not validate certificates. Its caller must verify the certificate before execution. The eventual experiment/production integration must be checked for that call-site obligation. Deterministic synthetic keys, in-process objects, abstract atomic durability, and isolated namespaces are explicitly conditional modeling devices, not a deployed security boundary.
- Online stopping occurs before request construction and dispatch. Cached raw records are replayed; orphan dispatch intents stop further sending rather than authorizing a retry. Disjoint replicate identities prevent cross-stream cache reuse. Fresh online/fixed streams may differ stochastically, so equality of completion must not be inferred from witness extension validity. Matched-prefix replay is a different, secondary estimand.
- Graph and padding prompts are locally token matched with a disclosed surrogate tokenizer. Their shared public fields and next-query schema are matched; actual tokenizer equivalence is not established. Protocol extraction fails closed, uses a unique explicit final message, and cannot turn historical responses into missing adaptive continuations.
- New runner paths target Phase 11 outputs and copy the inherited ledger prefix. No historical raw-record mutation was found in the inspected paths. This review did not independently rehash every old tracked file; the promised full baseline comparison remains a release gate.

## Research interpretation

The arithmetic `24 families × 2 models × 4 cells = 192 streams` is coherent as a prospectively chosen synthetic grid. It is **not** 192 independent samples: each contrast has at most 24 independent family units, and only one chosen lexical variant/actual world per family enters this API study. Additional worlds, faults, budgets, variants, or scaling siblings do not create independent replication. There is no power or representativeness justification in the inspected design; frame this as a bounded, descriptive finite-benchmark study and show individual-family effects and uncertainty. Lack of statistical significance is not evidence of equivalence or no completion loss.

The certificate propositions are elementary finite-set consequences of the declared explicit-receipt proof language. The implementation usefully distinguishes a common robust route from per-world feasibility and an intrinsic negative from unavailable information. The stated connections to decision-region identification, proof checking, guarded execution, and durable completion/fencing are appropriate in scope; this review did not perform a fresh literature search or verify the cited works. Do not claim new general active-testing theory, information-theoretic optimality outside this proof language, novel exactly-once execution, real-world security, or publication novelty. The credible potential contribution is the precise executable contract, independent semantic validation, transparent counterexamples, and measured synthetic tradeoffs.
