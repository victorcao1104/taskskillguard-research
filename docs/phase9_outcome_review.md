# Phase 9 independent outcome review

Date: 2026-10-16. Disposition: no correctness blocker found in the frozen experiment and saved outcomes. The evidence supports bounded claims about this synthetic corpus. It does not establish general completion dominance, real-world safety, or independent replication across 9,800 environments.

## Scope and integrity

This review follows the separate [prefreeze review](phase9_prefreeze_review.md). The three issues identified there were resolved before production freeze. After production and data freezes, the reviewer examined the heldout generator and reference artifacts, acquisition records, query results, runtime jobs and traces, offline analysis, and all five figures. No API, network, Git, or new container execution was performed. No frozen implementation, data, prompt, result, or prior-phase file was edited. This document is the outcome reviewer's only new repository artifact.

Independent recomputation established:

- All 29,195 baseline inventory files remained unchanged; production and data freeze checks passed.
- The first-pass heldout and truth hashes matched the final artifacts. First-pass and final coverage and novelty audits were byte-identical.
- Re-executing the 3,728 interface, 3,864 recovery, and 2,208 integrated jobs in memory reproduced both saved VM and container results byte-for-byte: 9,800 conditions in total.
- Rebuilding all 336 acquisition jobs reproduced their frozen bytes. Raw request identities, replicate identities, parsed observations, and per-job observation artifacts matched the saved records.
- All 672 query evaluations reproduced from the saved acquired orders or registered controls. Every proved label, including intrinsic unknown, agreed with the independent interpreter.
- The focused independent-reference tests passed 46 tests; the final analysis tests passed 13 tests. These checks supplement the release owner's broader suite and container execution records.

## Reference independence and novelty

The new set contains 24 cases in 12 authored families, with two lexical variants per family, 46 worlds, and 144 static effect candidates. Cases have four to eight effect candidates, with a median of six. The generator's project imports are the independent `truth_v6` and `truth_v7` implementations. Reference regeneration tests passed. The saved semantic differential covers 96 cases, 168 worlds, and 438 sites, with all 1,484 comparisons equal.

The novelty audit compared against 120 prior originals and 266 prior original/alternative programs. There were no normalized exact matches to the prior pool. The 12 within-new duplicate groups were precisely the intended lexical-variant pairs. The median nearest token distance was 37.5 against old originals and 36 against all prior programs. These are useful syntactic nonduplication checks; they do not establish semantic independence, out-of-distribution difficulty, or novelty of every alternative.

All four typed outcomes occur on each label axis. Nevertheless, authorization has only four `no` sites and current-route necessity only six, so minority-class conclusions have limited support. Sixteen multiworld cases are distinguishable using permitted observations. Four cases, in the staged-accumulator and canonical-key-alias families, retain hidden actual-world label disagreement without permitted pre-execution observations. Ten cases have no alternative route. These are meaningful structural differences, but the two variants and repeated world/fault conditions remain dependent observations.

Invalid factorial records had empty direct and reduced label maps, so both reducers receive the same invalid-response penalty. Analysis retains all planned candidate-axis denominators and reports validity-conditional accuracy separately.

The factorial comparison correctly applies both reducers to the same response and crosses deduplication and supplied tool facts. The reducer contrast is dependent postprocessing, not an independent acquisition. The tool-fact contrast includes full solver computation and more input context; it must not be called equal-information or equal-compute. ## Conditional observations and preflight

The global typed unknown remains distinct from a runtime-safe mixture of authorized and inactive sites. Planning admits only publicly supported possibilities; preflight probes all remaining hypotheses. The review found no actual-world or future-fault input crossing that planning boundary. Initial recovery selections were identical across fault schedules at otherwise identical settings.

For the new cases with preflight, the aggregate gate completes 104/184 conditions. Conditional gating with zero observations also completes 104/184; one observation raises completion to 128/184, and a second adds nothing. The 24 paired gains consist of eight write-through-pointer and 16 guarded-pair-join conditions, all using exactly one observation. Thus the observed gain is attributable to permitted information acquisition, concentrated in two of 12 families. The family-mean gain is 9.72 percentage points, with an exploratory interval from zero to 23.61 points; the raw condition gain is 24/184. These are different estimands.

Preflight has material negative results. At observation budget two:

- Aggregate gating loses 42 old/regression and eight new conditions, with no gains. All new losses are guarded-pair-join world 1.
- Conditional gating gains eight and loses 16 old/regression conditions, and gains four with no losses on new cases. Every old conditional loss is nested-noop-observer world 0.

Trace inspection explains these losses without an implementation defect. Robust feasibility can reject a route that succeeds in the favorable actual world. Authorization-focused observations do not necessarily investigate uncertainty that concerns only the goal. In other cases, disabling preflight permits paid reads before a gate blocks an effect; the read narrows beliefs and makes an alternative feasible. Early robust rejection prevents that information-gathering path. These are consequences of the registered policy and information timing, so preflight should not be described as universally improving completion.

## Recovery, receipts, cache, and accounting

Reconstruction from initial files and committed trace events matched final files and emissions. Every committed event was authorized, no receipt key committed twice, charged calls matched charged trace entries, and quotas held. Every ambiguous-outcome event was the final trace event. Planning units equaled counted planner steps plus primitive simulations and never exceeded the declared budget. Audit and search components reconciled separately.

Receipt reuse is materially exercised. The interface grid contains 837 reused-read events, including 13 whose current file contents differ from the saved receipt return. Those stale-return events preserve the frozen gateway semantics. The new recovery grid has 1,424 reused-read events but no stale read reuse; stale-read safety therefore has regression/toy support rather than a new heldout challenge in that grid.

The new fault schedules actually activate. Each schedule has 552 conditions. Triggered-condition counts are 384 for transient-before, known-after, double-transient, ambiguous-after, and mixed-known-then-ambiguous, and 192 for late-ambiguous. Mixed schedules reach their ambiguous event in 296 conditions; late ambiguity occurs in 192. Conditions that never dispatch because no route is feasible are not evidence of fault tolerance.

The policy and cache comparisons preserve their tradeoffs:

- Calls ranking versus priority has 32 completion wins and no losses over 1,288 pairs. Among 584 jointly successful pairs it uses 736 fewer calls, but incurs 976 more units of synthetic risk.
- Risk ranking versus calls has no wins and 24 completion losses over 1,288 pairs. All losses occur under late ambiguity. Among 592 jointly successful pairs it saves 1,736 risk units but uses 480 more calls. Shorter routes can finish before a dispatch-position fault; the risk surrogate is not a calibrated failure probability.
- Cache on versus off changes neither completion nor actual calls across 1,932 pairs. It saves 912 planning units. Only 48 rows have cache hits, confined to pointer-chain-fold under known-after or mixed schedules.
- There are 24 planning-budget exhaustions, all pointer-chain-fold with double-transient at budget 128. None occur at budget 512. The exhaustion is recorded without a budget overrun.

These results establish functional policy differences and limited cache reuse. They do not support broad claims that risk ranking improves reliability or that caching improves task completion.

## Analysis and claim limits

The offline scorer uses independent truth, preserves invalid responses, distinguishes proved intrinsic unknown from unresolved budget unknown, and requires matched runtime keys for paired contrasts. Costs over all conditions and costs conditional on both policies succeeding are separate. The 10,000-resample, seed-9009 bootstrap averages within case and family and gives each of the 12 families equal weight. Its intervals are exploratory and unadjusted for multiplicity; repeated faults, worlds, variants, and replicate acquisitions do not enlarge the number of independent families.

The five figures were visually inspected and match those denominator and cost distinctions. A final cross-check of `phase9_report.md` and `PHASE9.md` found no material quantitative overclaim. In particular, the report correctly describes Luna Q0's 4.125-query improvement in first full resolution as a potential stopping position, not a realized saving by the fixed-prefix executor. It retains the negative preflight and risk results, the absence of an integrated completion gain from better query allocation, and the narrow family concentration of observation and cache benefits. CI and final release verification remain the release owner's subsequent checks.

Planning accounting covers interpreter calls and entered steps, not all host CPU work. Parsing, key construction, and observation-separation search remain outside that metric. Finite public hypotheses, trusted local query provenance, synthetic grants and receipts, modeled latency, and fixed risk weights are assumptions of the experiment. Within those limits, no outcome-driven repair or unresolved correctness issue was found.
