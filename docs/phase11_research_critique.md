## Recommendation

Narrow the paper to **checkable evidence contracts for bounded safe execution under incomplete observations**. The potentially useful contribution is the concrete interface connecting acquired evidence, a precisely specified execution decision, and safe continuation after ambiguous outcomes. Neither decision-relevant acquisition, stopping after sufficient information, conservative shielding, nor commit-status recovery is new by itself. A coherent synthesis is possible, but publication novelty remains unestablished.

Use one central question: *When can a finite executor stop acquiring evidence without changing its specified next decision, and what evidence is needed to resume safely after an ambiguous mutation?* Query stopping is the main result; recovery is a second instantiation of the same evidence-validity discipline. Keep phase-aware parsing as a controlled implementation detail. Remove graph benefits, risk-ranking superiority, generic prompt-injection protection, and LLM superiority from the central claims unless new independent evidence supports them.

Phase 10 already supplies useful negative results: model selectors did not robustly outperform the frozen heuristic; graph representation had no consistent benefit; risk policy changed sign across fault mechanisms. Do not bury these. The next phase must improve the scientific discrimination between explanations, not just expand scenario counts.

## A tractable theorem worth stating precisely

Freeze a finite hypothesis space H, a deterministic full-evidence decision function D, a fixed policy/grant version, remaining execution budget, program/catalog version, and current history/receipt state. An evidence prefix E induces nonempty compatible completions V(E). A certificate contains the exact binding above, a proposed decision d, the evidence references, and a finite derivation checked by K. The checker must not fetch missing evidence or invoke uncharged execution.

**Next-decision invariance theorem.** Assume (1) actual semantics are contained in H; (2) trusted observations are sound and bound to the applicable snapshot; (3) K accepting implies `for every h in V(E), D(h)=d`; and (4) remaining acquisition is read-only and only removes inconsistent hypotheses without changing executor state, budgets, policy, or tie-breaking. Then every valid extension E' of E yields the same full-evidence decision d. Therefore acquisition can stop for this decision without changing D.

Proof: soundness retains the actual hypothesis; refinement gives nonempty `V(E') ⊆ V(E)`; D is constant on the latter set. This is intentionally elementary. Its scientific value must come from a nontrivial, sound, cheaper executable checker and adversarial evaluation, not the mathematical observation alone.

Three distinctions prevent overclaiming:

1. A certificate that merely proves a chosen route safe in every compatible world establishes **safe permissibility**, not equality with a full-information optimizer. If another route might be cheaper, safety can hold while decision equivalence fails.
2. Equality of the next action does not prove equality of complete executions, goal completion, cost, or recovery after future faults. For a trace-equivalence theorem, require a simulation/bisimulation invariant at every reachable decision and recertify after observations, commits, quota changes, or failures. Prefer the narrower theorem first.
3. If query execution consumes the same quota or planning budget as D, the theorem's no-state-change assumption fails. Include residual budgets in the decision or separately compare a budget-normalized reference. Additional information can also change route ordering even when safety is unchanged.

**Implementable finite checker direction.** Maintain unresolved evidence atoms, exact known values, and dependency sets for D's authorization/feasibility/ranking conditions. An acceptance certificate identifies a sufficient valuation of those conditions and proves every unresolved atom irrelevant to this specific decision, including deterministic ties. Exhaustive enumeration on tiny cases can validate soundness against a separately implemented D. If checking every completion costs the same as executing the omitted queries, expose that cost: the certificate has not established efficiency.

**Minimal stopping counterexamples.** (a) Two worlds agree that route A is authorized but differ in a goal precondition; treating authorization completeness as feasibility completeness can select a failing route. (b) Both worlds make A safe, but B is cheaper in one; a safe-route certificate does not preserve D. (c) A later quota/policy change invalidates the certificate even if its evidence bytes remain authentic. (d) Same first action followed by a fault can produce different suffixes; next-action invariance alone does not prove end-to-end equality.

## Recovery: essential additional assumptions

The most urgent issue is distinguishing **terminal noncommit** from **not yet committed**. Counterexample: a request is delayed; a fresh status read reports no committed record; the controller retries; the retry commits; the original later commits. Freshness alone did not prevent duplicate effects.

For a finite recovery lemma, bind every operation to an immutable run/operation identity, payload digest, destination, policy context, and epoch. Require an authoritative durable record atomically coupled to the effect; receipt retention throughout the permitted retry interval; and either (i) deduplication of all retries under that identity or (ii) terminal noncommit that fences the old attempt. A local synchronous simulator may assume no in-flight operations, but must state and test that restricted semantics rather than imply distributed safety.

**Conditional recovery lemma.** Starting from a history satisfying authorization and at-most-once invariants, verified COMMITTED evidence imports the existing receipt without dispatching the effect again. Verified terminal NOT_COMMITTED permits a fresh authorized dispatch only under the deduplication/fencing premise and remaining quota. UNKNOWN, UNAVAILABLE, PENDING, STALE, ID/payload/epoch mismatch, contradictory evidence, or exhausted status budget cannot justify resubmission. Induction over these transitions preserves the invariants. Liveness requires eventual usable evidence and sufficient budget; it is not guaranteed by safety.

A committed write proves that the write happened, not that its value is still current. Concurrent overwrite/deletion must invalidate a goal inferred from present file contents unless the experiment explicitly excludes concurrency. An emit's commit semantics must identify the observable effect being counted (gateway acceptance versus delivery); do not silently upgrade one into the other. A status query cannot grant new authority, restore consumed mutation quota, or certify unrelated actions.

## Experiments that discriminate mechanisms

Freeze production and analysis before exposing at least 32 independently authored **structural families**. More seeds, constants, paths, world expansions, faults, and budgets do not create independent families. Use a documented structure taxonomy and compare it with the previous 12-family set; if the new set is mostly parameterized old structures, report that honestly. Independent authoring reduces shared implementation bias but does not establish population sampling or statistical independence.

Primary stopping comparisons, holding query order and executor fixed: fixed budget; Phase 10 all-world authorization stopping; new decision certificate; no-stop matched checker overhead. Secondary acquisition comparisons: frozen heuristic, cost-aware decision-disagreement pair elimination, random with multiple fixed seeds, and a small exhaustive optimal acquisition oracle on tiny instances only. Do not compare only to full diagnosis, an intentionally stronger target. Keep the oracle out of production evidence and account for its offline nature.

For recovery compare: conservative stop-on-ambiguity; bounded status confirmation; a standard same-ID deduplication control if the modeled backend supports it; omniscient status only as a clearly labeled ceiling; unsafe blind retry only in the synthetic simulator as a diagnostic counterexample. Cross committed/uncommitted/pending with unavailable/stale/mismatch/contradictory status, retention expiry, competing routes, quota exhaustion, and failures before/after dispatch. Include unreachable fault targets in unconditional results, with reached-target strata separately reported.

Use a 2×2 factorial for decision stopping on/off and status recovery on/off under the same executor, budgets and fault seeds. This distinguishes two main effects from synergy. If the interaction is negligible, describe two complementary mechanisms sharing a contract rather than a unified algorithmic advance.

Primary outcomes: any decision mismatch against the declared reference; unauthorized effects; duplicate logical effects; completion including invalid/abstained runs; unique acquisition calls/steps; certificate checking cost; status calls; actual mutation calls. Separate actual paid API spend, offline replay, and counterfactual deployment costs. A cheap query counter with expensive uncharged certification is not an efficiency result. Record wall time as descriptive context alongside the abstract work metric.

Hold out adversarial negative-control families where all evidence is decision-relevant, no allowed query distinguishes the worlds, no safe route exists, the certificate offers no saving, or confirmation costs exceed saved work. Include certificate mutations (missing world, forged provenance, altered payload/program hash, stale epoch, omitted tie condition) that must reject. Require an independent small-state reference checker, not two wrappers around production semantics.

Predeclare one primary paired family-level contrast per mechanism and retain all families, failures, protocol invalidity, and exhausted budgets. Report distributions and maximum family loss as well as mean differences; bootstrap intervals are descriptive for this synthetic design, not a guarantee of real-world generalization. No human labels are necessary: independent executable reference semantics and exhaustive tiny-state checks are the relevant ground truth.

Spend the remaining budget on discrimination, not a large model/protocol grid. Validate deterministic mechanisms and controls first at zero API cost. If model selection remains in scope, use a small prespecified two-selector comparison on untouched families, include paid requests in cost, and retain the possibility that the deterministic baseline wins. Live adaptive acquisition is needed to claim actual API savings; replayed full streams establish deployment-scenario savings only.

## Claim boundaries and stop criteria

Potential defensible result: “Within a declared finite synthetic language and trusted observation/status contracts, a checked evidence interface preserves the specified next execution decision, and can reduce acquired evidence on some families; a separate receipt-confirmation transition safely recovers some ambiguous operations under stated backend assumptions.” Replace “can” with measurements only after collecting them.

Do not claim new active-learning theory, optimal stopping, general transaction recovery, comprehensive prompt-injection defense, production-ready Skill security, or real-world utility from these experiments. If all savings disappear when checker work is counted, if equivalence requires a full hidden audit, or if recovery requires an omniscient status oracle absent from the modeled API, narrow to a negative-result/reproducibility paper rather than retrofit the benchmark.

The strongest paper contribution may be exposing the gap between diagnostic completeness, safe permissibility, decision equivalence, and recoverable commit knowledge, with executable counterexamples and careful cost accounting. That is more credible than promising a positive LLM result.
