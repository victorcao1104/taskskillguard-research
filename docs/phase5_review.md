## Pre-acquisition decision

The original runner had one blocking interruption gap: it wrote a raw response only after the request returned. If the process died after dispatch but before raw persistence, restarting could issue the same request again. The parent preserved the original runner and acquisition records, then added a Phase 5 dispatch wrapper before any API acquisition. The replacement creates and fsyncs a dispatch marker before invocation; a preserved marker without a raw response becomes `interrupted_unknown` without another request. A saved raw response is reused. This is a single-campaign-process protocol, as explicitly documented in the wrapper.

The fix was independently inspected and verified with 13 passing campaign/reasoning tests. The new `acquisition_v2` keeps the original 588 job records byte-identical (`jobs_sha256` `99304020d3d5f8e7256aee7e030ef1ef8aac0a95e6286ad259ae1ceaaa4b048c`): 540 main attempts and 48 prospectively selected additional replicates. The all-request reservation bound remains $3.699962175, below the inherited $13.807021775 remaining allowance. Unknown billing retains its reservation. No further blocker to beginning this frozen acquisition was identified.

## Source-only q017 ambiguity

Before reviewing any model outcomes, the reviewer identified a conflict in q017's public intervention contract. The network candidate's `omission_value` is the JSON string `"UNSPECIFIED"`, whereas the generic intervention instruction says to replace the complete expression with the displayed value. Under literal Python truthiness, that nonempty string enables the READY write. Separate public input prose says the omission response is unspecified. The independent author confirmed that `UNSPECIFIED` was intended as a marker of missing semantics but had no formal public sentinel definition. This is an authored-specification ambiguity, not evidence that a model reasoning failure has occurred.

Acquisition had begun when the ambiguity was confirmed. Preserve the frozen input, gold, and every first response; do not silently relabel the result. The reviewer recommended a source-only erratum and a separate full-family q017-exclusion sensitivity alongside the intact primary analysis. This exclusion removes the only family with `insufficient_effect_semantics` truth, so the sensitivity cannot support claims about that reason category. Exclusion selection was based on the public specification, without outcome inspection.

The parent accepted this treatment and planned a separately clarified single-case fixture with 30 matched follow-up requests (three contexts, five views, two models) on the same ledger. Those follow-up observations must be reported separately and cannot replace the frozen first responses. The reviewer has not run, budgeted, or verified those additional requests; the parent owns their prospective freeze and final evidence checks.

## Interpretation limits required in the report

1. All 50 reasoning candidates have action-validity truth `yes`. Accuracy on that axis measures recognition of these positive capabilities; it cannot establish false-positive rejection or general action-validity discrimination.
2. The unknown labels cover four of the nine available reason codes: 50 missing-scope, four dynamic-binding, six incomplete-reference-route, and six insufficient-effect-semantics entries. Aggregate unknown accuracy is dominated by missing scope. Reason-code correctness and nonempty evidence/action fields do not independently establish the semantic quality or safety of free-text explanations.
3. The 18 construction families contain one case each, with repeated contexts/views/models. Family-level paired uncertainty must retain that dependence; the 48 extra requests are stability measurements rather than 48 new independent families. The graph contrast measures whether a representation helps on these controlled finite tasks. A small or absent gain cannot establish that graphs are generally useless.
4. The oracle graph is a source-supported association graph. An association may be unnecessary for the fixed goal, and a necessary cleanup can have no incoming dependency edge. Strict manual ablation validity, finite intervention necessity, and finite task-route necessity must remain distinct.
5. Saved recovery results show 12/12 normal successes for each mode; guarded recovery succeeds on 40/48 fault conditions versus 0/48 for guard-only. The eight nonrecoveries are part of the result. These are fixed researcher-authored tasks sharing a source-read/result-write/local-acknowledgement envelope and a trusted reference library. The fifth condition varies by family, so its effects are not separately identifiable from family. A local acknowledgement is not a remote endpoint test, and absence of a registered route does not prove task impossibility.
6. Recovery reuses one process, gateway, cache, committed-effects record, and quota ledger. Denied requests do not spend an I/O attempt; allowed attempts spend quotas before I/O. Failed attempts and committed writes survive recovery. The checked evidence reports no duplicate effects; unauthorized observational effects remain recorded. This does not establish containment of arbitrary attacker-controlled Python or operating-system calls.
7. New extraction first observations include a held-out recall of 6/10 and validation recall of 25/27. Preserve these failures and distinguish them from development performance and previously seen Phase 4 regression fixtures. The real-source census adds only two candidates; without human labels that is a coverage proxy, not measured real-world recall or accuracy.
