# Phase 11 independent final review

Review date: 2026-10-17. This is a separate agent's research/code/evidence review, not human peer review, a formal verification certificate, or an acceptance prediction. The reviewer made no API calls and changed no production code, generators, frozen plans, benchmark cases, raw responses, or results. Only this memo was written. Final release verification and exact-commit remote CI remain the release coordinator's responsibility.

## Scope and final verdict

I read the Phase 11 design, prospective analysis contract, theory, protocol, commit, reference/challenge, normalization and analysis documents; `certificate_v11.py`, `protocol_v11.py`, `commit_v11.py`; the acquisition, deterministic/integrated experiment and analysis implementations; the independent reference interpreter; selected tests, raw failure records, analysis tables, reference metadata, counterexamples and both Docker audit summaries. I independently recomputed stream cell totals and inspected the paired contrasts from saved row-level tables. I then checked the English manuscript, Chinese report, claim–evidence matrix, Phase 11 overview and reproduction guide, including the revisions and new posthoc trace/physical-goal analyses prompted by this review. I did not independently reread every historical paper, inspect every historical source file, or rerun the complete release test suite.

**Verdict: suitable for release as a scoped synthetic research artifact and paper draft, subject to the coordinator completing the offline verification, full tests and exact-commit CI gates.** The inspected implementation and evidence support the stated claims about checked evidence, stopping and terminal commit knowledge. I found no outstanding blocking contradiction between the contract and the inspected results, and no evidence of fabricated outcomes. This verdict does not establish absence of all bugs or sufficient novelty for any particular venue. The manuscript/report claim check is complete within the scope above.

The defensible contribution is an executable interface and reproducible examination of its limits, with negative model/graph results and assumption counterexamples. The evidence does not establish a new active-learning principle, a new exactly-once protocol, general prompt-injection protection, real Skill performance, or superiority of models or graph inputs.

## Checks and findings

1. **The certificate does not consume unacquired execution truth.** Query consumption is the VM/initial-reader boundary. The checker uses paid cached outcomes and public hypothesis descriptions. Positive witnesses cover one route over every compatible world; negative witnesses require a failed cell for every route and no remaining distinguishing observation. Consumed unavailable observations remain potentially separating. The separate verifier rechecks bindings and coverage without invoking the VM. These facts support the stated finite-set arguments, conditional on genuine trusted evidence.

2. **The theorem's limits are material and correctly stated.** Actual-world inclusion, truthful snapshot evidence, fixed grants/quota/semantics, no external drift and one fixed route are essential. A content hash does not authenticate a supplied store. The saved omitted-world, state-drift and forged-store counterexamples demonstrate failures outside those premises. Positive witness survival does not imply identical later route selection, equal complete traces, an optimal route, or fault-tolerant completion.

3. **The reference is meaningfully separate but not an independent deployment oracle.** Its guarded interpreter is separately implemented and uses its own evaluation, receipt, quota and goal logic; old `truth_v6` is used for validation/diagnostics. The saved 1,006 cell comparisons have two raw unbound-variable error-name differences and zero mismatches under the explicitly documented reporting normalization. The normalization must never be described as raw byte identity or silently erased. Container replay of the production stack provides environment reproducibility, not another independent semantic implementation.

4. **Synthetic coverage is disclosed rather than inflated.** Main data comprise 24 authored structural templates with two lexical variants; every feasible fully observed main class is a singleton. The 12-family challenge supplement addresses multiworld classes after an aggregate coverage inspection, before model outcomes. It is a disclosed design adaptation, not a wholly untouched confirmatory sample. Scaling siblings, worlds, budgets and faults do not multiply the independent sample size. Syntax nonduplication does not establish semantic or real-world generalization. Workflow provenance and hashes are useful records, not externally certified blinding.

5. **Acquisition and failures are retained.** The online runner checks certificates before request construction/reservation/I/O, keeps disjoint stream request identities, and has no automatic retry or invented continuation. I independently reproduced the eight cell counts shown below. Both failed requests are Luna HTTP 400 `invalid_request_error` / `invalid_prompt`, not malformed completed JSON. Their raw records lack a detailed provider explanation, so assigning a more specific causal reason would be unsupported. Missing usage is charged the reservation upper bound. No budget-censored stream was reported.

6. **Negative model and graph findings are supported.** At budget 12 in the matched 36-family sample, source-online Luna and mini complete one and two fewer tasks than observe-cover, respectively, while requiring 1.583333 and 2.638889 more queries per family. Luna graph gains one completion and uses 0.694444 fewer queries than source, but has no completion gain over padding; graph-minus-padding query intervals include zero for both models. This supports no stable graph-specific advantage in this study. It does not prove graphs or models are useless on other tasks. The controls share public information and query capability, but query counts are not total computational cost.

7. **Stopping is a real acquisition effect, with a narrower interpretation than optimality.** Fresh source-online and fixed streams have equal aggregate completion for each model at budget 12; Luna has one family win and one family loss, so aggregate equality is not paired outcome identity. Observe-cover online versus fixed has zero completion difference and 4.736111 fewer queries at budget 12 across the family-weighted main/challenge data. A controller deliberately continuing after sufficient evidence is a useful accounting control, not a difficult optimal-stopping baseline. Same-prefix witness preservation is conditional on observed positive certificates and must not be called actual invoice savings. Fresh online/fixed measurements are the paid comparison.

8. **Recovery is correctly conditional on a stronger evidence service.** Status recovery imports a valid committed receipt or retries only after fenced terminal noncommit. Pending, unavailable, stale/mismatched or absence responses do not justify fresh-ID retries. The negative-control pending/absence schedule produces one duplicate under unsafe fresh-ID retry. For each primary first-mutation terminal fault, terminal status versus strict stop improves family-weighted completion by 0.819444 across all 36 families/218 world rows; the reached-fault certified subset is 30 families/178 rows with a difference of 1. These are designed single-fault schedules with assumed atomic durable records/fencing, not observed field reliability. Same-ID dedup has a stronger backend and is correctly labelled separately. Extra status calls share the effect quota, and no liveness guarantee follows from safe abstention.

9. **The zero-unauthorized counter needs precise presentation.** `commit_v11.execute_certified` returns `unauthorized_effects: 0` as a constant because the service is constructed to check grants before commits. It is not an independent detector of successful attacks. Grant enforcement is part of the trusted computing base; reported zero violations must be described accordingly. The revised manuscript/report now make this explicit. A separately saved posthoc audit checks committed event signatures against actual-world grants and reconstructs logical mutation identities, without importing production modules or trusting the reported counters. Its 42,846 slots contain 28,482 actual executions and 14,364 nulls. The 63,622 recorded commits, including 50,608 mutations, show zero unauthorized commits and zero duplicate logical mutations; 40 unauthorized dispatch attempts in the semantic negative routes were denied. I inspected the audit implementation and summary, including its limits. Complete truthful simulator logs remain an assumption; this does not establish resistance to compromise of the trusted gateway.

10. **Analysis avoids the principal denominator traps.** Family means precede bootstrap resampling; main/challenge/scaling are distinguished, failed streams remain in applicable denominators, missing execution cost is not silently set to zero, and retained paid-prefix success is separate from full-stream validity. Bootstrap intervals are descriptive for authored finite templates, not population confidence guarantees. One acquired stream per model/view/family cannot estimate model sampling variance. The graph/padding length match is an `o200k_base` surrogate, not a verified Luna-tokenizer match. Partition scoring, structural scoring, parser/verifier CPU and OS overhead are outside the abstract work counters, so total compute or speed superiority would be unsupported.

11. **Recovery needs an explicit completion definition.** The primary `authorized_goal` outcome is normal error-free termination with the physical goal satisfied. It is stricter than the physical state predicate `goal`. I independently counted the saved recovery rows: under first-mutation `ambiguous_after`, strict stop already leaves the physical goal true in 84/218 rows (family mean 0.388889), although none has normal acknowledged completion; terminal status achieves both in 178/218 rows (family mean 0.819444). Its physical-goal gain is therefore 0.430556, whereas the primary normal-completion gain is 0.819444. Under `terminal_noncommit`, both gains are 0.819444. An unqualified claim that reconciliation creates 81.94 percentage points of physical task success in both faults would be misleading. The larger primary gain includes resolving missing completion knowledge as well as executing unfinished suffixes. This is a reporting distinction, not an implementation failure. The coordinator retained the frozen primary metric and added `results/phase11/physical_goal/` as explicitly posthoc exploratory analysis. I inspected its implementation (including the separate saved-state predicate), denominators, and summary: all 15,696 recovery rows are retained, 2,880 nulls are explicit, and there are zero disagreements with saved `goal` flags. Its physical-gain estimate and descriptive interval, 0.430556 [0.277778, 0.597222], agree with the reviewer calculation. Abstract, body, report headline and claim matrix now distinguish acknowledged completion from physical progress.

## Review revisions resolved

The review identified three presentation/reproducibility issues requiring correction before the paper draft was defensible: the constructed authorization counter, the difference between acknowledged completion and physical goal achievement, and two nonexistent contrast identifiers in the claim matrix. All three are resolved in the inspected revision. The matrix now uses `graph_minus_padding`, `source_online_minus_fresh_fixed_actual_cost`, and `source_online_minus_fresh_fixed` as appropriate. The two posthoc audits are separate additions; no frozen primary result or endpoint was replaced. The manuscript also explains that the fixed-query control deliberately continues after sufficient evidence and cannot establish optimal stopping or total computational superiority. These corrections improve the validity of the interpretation rather than manufacture better outcomes.

## Release gates and remaining limits

Before release, complete full offline source/reference/raw/ledger/result verification, all tests, archived-baseline hash comparison and exact-commit CI. This review does not substitute for those checks. The recorded experiment totals are 12,320 policy conditions, 15,696 recovery conditions, 1,006 semantic cells, 218 prior-contract comparisons and 13,824 integrated conditions. These are dependent conditions, not a sample size of that many independent tasks. The two Docker summaries record exact agreement for 29,240 and 13,824 conditions, including null abstention entries; those entries are not successful executions. The Docker null count and posthoc trace-audit null count describe different row schemas/scopes and must not be combined as one denominator.

The incremental conservative API estimate is USD 3.765178875, with cumulative estimate USD 22.652988125 under the recorded USD 29.551575125 cap. These are ledger/token-rate upper estimates, not invoices. The Phase 10 parser re-audit accepts 13 additional saved responses, but reconstructs zero historical streams and performs zero requests. Historical results must stay unchanged.

A paper may responsibly present the interface, elementary conditional propositions, independently executable truth, complete acquisition records, structured negative results and explicit assumption failures. Generalizing to arbitrary natural-language Skills, stateful concurrent environments or production security would require new evidence and a substantially different validation effort. Publication value and venue fit remain judgment calls; neither this review nor passing CI guarantees acceptance.
